Privacy Policy

Casa Brolin · Last updated 7 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Casa Brolin ("we", "us") collects through casabrolin.com, our enquiry forms and our advertisements, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through our website or an advertisement, we collect the details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, and anything you write in the message or answer in the form. When you call or message us, we keep a record of that conversation. Our website also uses cookies and similar technology, including the Meta Pixel and Google Ads tags, which record visits and form submissions.

How we use it

We use your details to respond to your enquiry, share information about our villas, arrange site visits, and stay in touch by phone, WhatsApp, SMS or email about the property you asked about. We use website and advertising data to measure how our advertisements perform and to show our advertisements to people likely to be interested.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our customer records, calling, messaging and advertising platforms (including Meta and Google), and only as needed for the purposes above. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we are in contact with you about a property and for a reasonable period afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. Write to us at the address below and we will act on your request within 30 days. You can also control cookies through your browser settings and manage advertising preferences in your Facebook, Instagram and Google account settings.

Data deletion requests

To have your information deleted, email us from the address you used in your enquiry, or include the phone number you gave us, and ask for deletion. We will confirm once it is done.
